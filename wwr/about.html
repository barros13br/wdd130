History

Our rafting company began as a small family project, born from a love of rivers and outdoor adventure. Over the years,we expanded our routes and trained expert guides who share the same passion for nature. What started as weekend trips soon grew into a trusted service known for safety, excitement, and unforgettable experiences. Today, we continue to welcome adventurers from all backgrounds, helping them discover the thrill of the water.
